    The ball that moves

    We play with the Tilde swing ball. It's the reason a 6-over Test here feels like proper cricket.

    Tilde swing ball · yellow & pink

    One half of the ball is dimpled and the other half is smooth. That difference in air pressure makes it swing, so a bowler can move it in, move it out, or reverse it late, just like a hardball. But it weighs about 70 grams, the same as a taped tennis ball, so nobody needs pads and the box's walls survive the night.

    The bounce is true off astro-turf, and the surface stays the same from the first over to the last. Batters who are used to tape balls take a few overs to get used to it. Bowlers love it straight away.
